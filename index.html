INNNAANIMATTEE INSANITYYYY

I like talking about this show a lot it's been holding me captive for years.

MePhone and Fan have already been similar in their own ways. While fundamentally different in their own ways. While fundamentally different in personalities, their actions, mentality, and originality(?) is the same.

MePhone4 was created for a single purpose of generating items. Fan was created for the single purpose of being A fan of inanimate insanity. Both were made as something that wasn't meant to survive for long. [With mephone, it's the other phone models--and with fan, his origin meant to be a throw-away joke] Both even technically have direct ties with the shimmers.

Throughout the series, we see more and more on how alike they are. Their time on the show brings up their dependance on it all the time. Both MePhone and Fan use the show to escape reality. Or rather, to use this distraction as an attempted form of healing themselves. Most easily noticeable similarity is that they're both the hosts of ii. Fun fact! Fan has never died [besides through deletion in the finale trilogy], however, Fan and mephone had both been on the brink of death because of the show. MePhone especially multiple times.

And with one of the more recent episodes, iv6. This one is FUUULLLLLL of parallels. Another character, Marsh, has compared Fan to Cobs and Mephone ["MePhone's not the only one who created someone, wanting them to be a certain way, only for the creation to defy them.] multiple times. The recent episodes have made it even MORE clearer about their similarities specifically when the game was created as a fun thing meant for no harm to be done. Just pure whimsy.

But it was later turned into close calls with Meeple being the reason for the shutdown of said game and/or the need to run away for safety. MePhone had a habit of running away during his problems with Cobs catching up, and now Fan and the other contestants have the burden on THEM to run for safety and security away from meeple. The second Fan comes to from his coma, he tells everyone that they need to flee the island because they're supposedly being tracked down. The simple IDEA of a Cobs-like figure is too much of a risk to stay on the island.

Fan even started making the challenges turn from 'fun things for the love of the game' to 'we need to do this for the purpose of leaving the island or else we're all in danger'. Just like how MePhone would make his challenges specific to clean up his messes. [ex, saving Marsh from Mars, getting him a new battery when he was sick, saving Fan from aliens, defeating Cobs, etc.]

This reminds me of the thought mephone wouldve had when going back to s2. In the episode, Cabby mentioned "..But he's not looking for power. He has good intentions in a desperate situation." He wanted to fix everything [good intentions] but before he had the chance to do that, everyone around him was dying [desperate situation] So he did the only thing he could-hiding. [Or at least tried to with the closet before mepad got him out.] Which is what Fan is doing right now. [running away] For the good of the show.

And last thing I can think of on the spot: Bot.

Both MePhone and Fan look up to Bot in their own perspectives. MePhone likes the fact that Bot isn't bound by his code and got to create their own person. In a way, he sees a happier version of himself in them. Fan is also proud of them for the same reason, and it seems to the audience that sometimes, he wishes he could do the same. He tells Bot that they make it look so easy to break out of the patterns that were set for them, indirectly reffering to himself for being flawed by design, but unlike them, he couldn't betray the way he was written to be.
